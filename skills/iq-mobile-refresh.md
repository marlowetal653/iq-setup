Force a full refresh of the Expo app on the student's phone.

Steps:
1. Run:
   ```bash
   npx expo start --clear
   ```
   This clears the Metro bundler cache and restarts the dev server.

2. Tell the student: "Your app is refreshing! It might take a few seconds to reload on your phone. If it doesn't update automatically, shake your phone to open the Expo menu and tap **Reload**."
